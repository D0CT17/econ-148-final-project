Steps to get our project running!

1. Download our .zip file.
2. Go to Google Colab.
3. In the top left click 'File'.
4. Then click 'Upload Notebook'.
5. Go into our .zip file and drag the .ipynb file named "CODING PROJECT HERE" into the upload square, do NOT attempt through "Browse", do NOT upload the .zip file.
6. Click the folder icon named 'Files' in the middle left if 'Files' tab is not already open. 
7. Wait until folder named "sample_data" shows up, do NOT click it.
8. Drag all of the .csv files into the space on the under 'Files', do NOT upload the .zip here.
9. Hit "Run all" at the top and with a little prayer it should work.
